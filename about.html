About Jul's Auto Repair

Your trusted local auto repair shop on Route 44 in Raynham, Massachusetts.

Built on trust, since 2026

Jul's Auto Repair opened with one idea: honest, reliable repair at a fair price for the Raynham community. We saw drivers tired of being a number, upsold on parts they didn't need and rushed out the door.

So we built the opposite. Every customer is treated like a neighbor, because most of them are. We tell you what your car needs, show you why, and do the work right the first time.

Located on US-44 in Raynham, we serve drivers from Raynham, Taunton, Bridgewater, Norton, Easton, Middleborough, Mansfield, Lakeville and the surrounding southeastern Massachusetts towns.
